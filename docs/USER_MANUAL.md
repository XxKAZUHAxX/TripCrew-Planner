Welcome to **TripCrew** — the app that helps you and your friends or family finally agree on a group trip! This guide explains everything you need to know to use the app, step by step. No technical knowledge required.

## 1. What TripCrew Does

Planning a trip with a group is hard — everyone wants something different, nobody wants to decide, and dates never seem to line up. TripCrew solves this with a simple flow:

1. Someone (the **host**) creates a trip and invites the group.
2. Everyone suggests destinations they'd like to visit.
3. Everyone votes on their favorites.
4. If there's a tie or people don't vote in time, a fun **spinning wheel** picks the winner.
5. Everyone marks the dates they're free on a shared calendar.
6. Once the destination is decided, the host shares the plan and everyone tracks what they need to prepare.

Along the way, the app also hands out silly "badges" based on how you behave in the group (like being the last one to vote).

---

## 2. Creating Your Account

1. Open TripCrew in your browser.
2. Click **Register** (or **Sign Up**).
3. Fill in:
    - Your **name**
    - Your **email address**
    - A **password** (at least 6 characters)
4. Click **Create Account**. You'll be logged in automatically and taken to your trips list.

> Keep your password somewhere safe — there is no "forgot password" recovery, so choose something memorable.

---

## 3. Logging In & Out

**To log in:**

1. Go to the **Login** page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Log In**.

**To log out:**

- Click **Logout** in the top navigation bar. You'll be returned to the login screen.

**Staying logged in:**

- You'll normally stay logged in for about a week. After that, you'll simply be asked to log in again — nothing is lost.

---

## 4. Creating a Trip

1. After logging in, go to **My Trips**.
2. Fill in the **new trip** form:
    - **Title** — e.g., "Summer Beach Trip 2026" (required)
    - **Voting deadline** — the date and time by which everyone must vote (optional, but recommended so voting doesn't drag on forever)
3. Click **Create**.

You're automatically the **Host** of this trip, and the trip appears on your dashboard.

**What does being the Host mean?**
As the host, you have extra powers other members don't have:

- Editing the trip's title and dates
- Managing the invite link
- Ending voting early
- Spinning the Wheel of Destiny (if needed)
- Deleting the trip
- Writing the trip's instructions in the Playbook

---

## 5. Inviting Your Friends

1. Open your trip's dashboard.
2. You'll see an **invite link** — something like `https://yourapp.com/join/abc123`.
3. Click **Copy** and share it with your group via text, chat, or however you like.
4. Anyone with the link can join, as long as it hasn't been turned off (see below).

**Turning the invite link off (host only):**
If you don't want new people joining anymore, the host can **deactivate** the link from the dashboard. You can turn it back **on** any time — the link itself never changes, just whether it currently works.

---

## 6. Joining a Trip

1. Click the invite link a friend sent you.
2. If you're not logged in yet, you'll be asked to log in or register first — then you'll be brought right back to the invite.
3. You'll see a short preview: the trip's name and how many people have already joined.
4. Click **Join This Trip**.

If you're already a member, clicking the link just takes you straight to the trip's dashboard.

---

## 7. Suggesting Places to Go

While voting is open, anyone in the trip can suggest a destination:

1. Go to the trip dashboard.
2. In the **Propose a Destination** section, fill in:
    - **Name** — e.g., "Tokyo, Japan" (required)
    - **Description** — any extra notes (optional)
    - **Budget tier** — choose **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**
3. Click **Add**. It appears in the destination list immediately for everyone to see.

**Removing a suggestion:**
You can remove any destination you personally proposed. The host can remove **any** destination. Removing a destination after people have already voted for it is fine — it just won't count anymore.

---

## 8. Voting for Your Favorite Destination

Once there are destinations to choose from, everyone ranks them in order of preference.

1. Go to the **Vote** page for the trip.
2. You'll see all proposed destinations in an **Unranked** list.
3. Click **Add to ranking** on each place you want to include, in the order you prefer them.
4. Use the **up/down arrows** to reorder your list, or the **✕** to remove a place from your ranking.
5. Click **Save Vote**.

**How the scoring works (in plain terms):**
Your top choice gets the most points, your second choice gets slightly fewer, and so on. Anything you leave unranked gets zero points from you. All members' points are added together, and the destination with the highest total is winning. You can see live results update on the scoreboard as soon as you save.

**Can I change my vote?**
Yes! Go back to the Vote page any time before voting closes and update your ranking. There's no limit to how many times you can change your mind (though changing it a _lot_ earns you a joke badge — see below).

**A heads-up before saving:**
If you save a vote that doesn't rank every destination, the app will double-check with you, since unranked items get zero points. You can go back and finish ranking, or confirm and save anyway.

**When voting closes:**
Once the trip's destination is decided, the Vote page becomes **view-only** — you can see everyone's final rankings, but you can no longer change your vote.

## 9. The Trip Dashboard

The dashboard is your trip's home base. It shows:

- The trip's **name** and current **status**:
    - 🗳️ _Voting in progress_
    - ✅ _Destination decided_
    - 📦 _Archived_
- A **live scoreboard** ranking destinations by votes so far.
- The **voting deadline** — switching to a countdown once less than 48 hours remain.
- Who has **voted** already, and a running tally like "3 of 5 members have voted."
- The **members list**, along with everyone's earned badges.
- All **suggested destinations** with their budget levels.
- Quick links to **Vote**, **Availability**, the **Wheel of Destiny** (when relevant), and the **Playbook**.

Just revisit this page any time to see the latest scores and updates — there's no need to refresh manually, though you can reload the page whenever you want the newest information.

## 10. Fun Badges

TripCrew automatically hands out light-hearted badges based on how members behave during a trip. These aren't permanent — they're calculated fresh every time you view the dashboard, and they're specific to each trip. You can hold more than one at a time, and yes, you can see your own.

| Badge                   | How you earn it                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 👑 **The Dictator**     | Suggested more than 5 destinations yourself.                              |
| 👻 **The Ghost**        | Haven't voted yet, and the deadline is less than 24 hours away.           |
| 🧮 **The Accountant**   | Suggested 2 or more destinations, and every one of them was "Low" budget. |
| 🤔 **The Overthinker**  | Changed your vote more than 3 times.                                      |
| 🎉 **The Hype Machine** | Were the very first person in the group to vote.                          |

## 11. Picking Dates Everyone Is Free

Deciding _where_ to go is only half the battle — TripCrew also helps you find _when_ everyone's available.

1. Go to the trip's **Availability** page.
2. You'll see a calendar for the current month. Use the **arrows** to look up to 6 months ahead.
3. **Click or tap** a date to mark yourself as free that day. **Click and drag** (or swipe on mobile) across several dates to select or deselect a whole range at once.
4. Your chosen dates are outlined in blue.
5. Your selections **save automatically** — there's no separate save button.

**Reading the calendar (the heatmap):**
Each date's background color shows how many people are free that day:

| Color       | Meaning               |
| ----------- | --------------------- |
| White       | Nobody's free yet     |
| Light green | 1 person free         |
| Teal        | 2 people free         |
| Dark green  | 3 or more people free |

**Finding the best date quickly:**
Below the calendar, open the **Best Dates for Your Group** panel. It lists the top dates ranked by how many people are free, and hovering over a date shows exactly who's available. This saves you from having to scan the whole calendar by eye.

## 12. When Voting Ends in a Tie: The Wheel of Destiny

Sometimes the group can't agree, or people don't vote in time. That's where the **Wheel of Destiny** comes in — a fun spinning wheel that randomly (and fairly) breaks the tie.

**When does it appear?**
The host will see a **Spin the Wheel** button when either:

- The top two destinations are tied in votes, or
- The voting deadline has passed and fewer than half the group has voted.

**How to use it:**

1. The host goes to the Wheel page and clicks **Spin the Wheel of Destiny**.
2. The wheel spins and lands on a destination — this outcome is decided fairly by the system before the animation even plays, so there's no way to "cheat" the spin.
3. Once it stops, a **winner banner** appears for the whole group.

**If you're not the host:**
You'll see a message like "Waiting for [host] to spin the wheel…" You can hit **Refresh** any time, and the page also checks automatically every few seconds — you'll see the result appear without needing to do anything once it's spun.

**What happens after:**
The trip is marked as **decided**, the winning destination is locked in, and the **Playbook** unlocks for everyone.

> If the host doesn't spin within 12 hours after the deadline, the wheel spins automatically so the group isn't left waiting.

## 13. The Trip Playbook (Planning the Details)

Once a destination is finalized (either by voting or the wheel), the **Playbook** unlocks. This is where the group organizes the actual logistics.

**If you're the host:**

1. Go to the Playbook page and click **Edit**.
2. Write your instructions — meeting points, itinerary, packing tips, anything the group needs. You can use simple formatting like headings, bold text, bullet points, and links.
3. Click **Save Instructions**. Everyone will now see the nicely formatted version.

**For everyone in the trip:**

- View the host's instructions and the winning destination at the top of the page.
- Add **checklist tasks** for the group — e.g., "Book flights," "Buy travel insurance," "Pack sunscreen."
- Check off tasks as **you personally** complete them. Everyone tracks their own progress independently — checking a box only affects your own status, not anyone else's. You'll also see how many people in total have completed each task.
- Delete a task if you created it (or if you're the host).

> The Playbook only becomes available after the destination is decided. If you try to visit it while voting is still open, you'll be told to wait.

---

## 14. Managing Your Trip (For the Host)

As the host, you have a few extra tools on the dashboard:

- **Edit trip details** — change the title, dates, or voting deadline at any time.
- **Conclude voting now** — end voting early instead of waiting for the deadline. If there's a clear winner, the group moves straight to the Playbook. If it's a tie, the Wheel of Destiny becomes available.
- **Toggle the invite link** — turn it off to stop new people from joining, or back on to allow it again.
- **Delete the trip** — permanently removes the trip and all its data (destinations, votes, availability, playbook) for everyone. You'll be asked to confirm since this cannot be undone.

---

## 15. Leaving a Trip

If you're a regular member (not the host) and want to leave a trip:

1. Go to the trip dashboard.
2. Click **Leave Trip**.
3. Confirm when asked.

You'll be removed from the trip and it will no longer show up in your trip list. Note: the host cannot leave a trip — if the trip is no longer needed, the host should delete it instead.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

**I can't log in — it says my session expired.**
Your login naturally expires after about a week for security. Just log in again with your email and password — nothing about your trips or votes is lost.

**Why can't I access the Playbook?**
The Playbook only unlocks once the group's destination has been decided (either through voting or the Wheel of Destiny). While voting is still open, this page stays locked.

**Why is the "Spin the Wheel" button not showing up?**
It only appears when there's an actual tie in the scores, or the voting deadline has passed with too few votes. Otherwise, the group simply proceeds with the destination that has the most votes.

**I don't see any colors on the availability calendar.**
That simply means nobody has marked their availability yet. As soon as people start selecting dates, the calendar will light up with colors.

**Can I change my vote after saving it?**
Yes, as many times as you like — right up until the trip's destination is officially decided.

**What happens to votes for a destination that got deleted?**
Nothing bad — the trip continues normally. That destination just no longer earns any points going forward.

**I'm not the host — can I still add destinations or checklist tasks?**
Yes. Any member can suggest destinations (while voting is open) and add checklist tasks (once the Playbook is unlocked). Only removing _other people's_ destinations or tasks is restricted to the host or the original creator of that item.

**Is my information private from other members?**
Your specific votes, ranking changes, and availability selections are visible to other trip members so the group can plan together — that's the whole point of the app! There's no separate private mode for a shared group trip.
